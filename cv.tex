\item Developed an ultrasonic array test system equipped with multi-channel, high-frequency, and quick synchronous data acquisition, achieving millimeter-level accuracy in measuring bubble inside liquid nitrogen storage tanks at approximately -196$^\circ$C (77K).
\end{joblong}


\begin{joblong}{Deep Learning-based Reconstruction of Transient Temperature Fields in Thrust Chambers --- Project Student Leader}{}
\item Innovated fluid field inversion by diffusion models (UNet and EDM frameworks), surpassing traditional neural networks in accuracy and achieving under 2\% average error in reconstructing temperature, velocity, and pressure fields from sparse measurements.
\item Developed a multi-condition dataset by an elliptic PDE-based grid, supporting 416 chemical cases, and introduced an SSIM-based metric with PAC\&I2SB methods for super resolution, enabling high fidelity reconstruction of transient thermoacoustic dynamics with 0.327\% relative error via DDIM.
\end{joblong}


\begin{joblong}{High-resolution and Ultrafast Characterization and Diagnosis of Thermochemical Reactions under Extreme Experimental Conditions --- Project Student Leader}{}
\item Ultra high speed optical measurement and high spatiotemporal resolution spectroscopy were employed to achieve dynamic measurement of the flame parameters under high temperature and high pressure, thereby revealing ultrafast reaction mechanisms in multiphysics interactions.
\item Laser interferometry and sub-pixel edge detection were utilized to capture microscale wall vibrations, leading to the proposal of corresponding control strategies.
\end{joblong}


\begin{joblong}{Multi-scale 3D PIV: From Nanoparticle Tracking to High-velocity Flows --- Project Student Leader}{}
\item Two sets of Particle Image Velocimetry (PIV) measurement systems, High pressure, high speed PIV system (6 MPa, 100-600 m/s) and 2D3C-PIV system can be used for three-dimensional measurement, high precision capture of nanoparticle motion trajectories in the flow field.
\end{joblong}
